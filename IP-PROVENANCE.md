# Project Provenance

This repository contains an independently developed engineering project based on a fictional IT service operations scenario.

The project is intended to demonstrate the design, implementation, testing, and operation of an enterprise-style AI application.

## Source Material

- Business scenarios, organizations, service cases, and test datasets are fictional or independently created.
- Project documentation, architecture decisions, and implementations are developed specifically for this repository.
- Confidential customer information, proprietary code, restricted documents, and private system configurations must not be included.

## Third-Party Components

Open-source libraries, frameworks, models, and other external resources may be used in accordance with their licenses and usage conditions.

Dependencies and externally sourced assets will be identified as they are introduced.

## Development Process

AI-assisted tools may be used during design, implementation, testing, and review. Generated suggestions are reviewed before they are accepted into the project.

Architectural decisions, implementation choices, and published engineering claims remain the responsibility of the project maintainer.

## Publication

Only material that is independently created or authorized for public distribution should be committed to this repository. Any unresolved ownership or licensing question must be settled before publication.
